# Favorite Series: Stranger Things

> *"Friends don't lie."* — Eleven

**Stranger Things** is a sci-fi horror drama series set in the 1980s town of Hawkins, Indiana, following a group of kids who uncover supernatural mysteries, secret experiments, and an alternate dimension called the *Upside Down*.

## Why I Like It
* **Strong Nostalgia:** Captures classic 1980s adventure vibes, synth-driven music, and arcade culture.
* **Character Growth:** The main cast grows up across seasons while keeping their friendship at the center of the story.
* **Suspense & Mystery:** Every season introduces high stakes, eerie plot twists, and memorable villains.

## Top Characters
1. **Eleven** – Telekinetic protagonist trying to understand the outside world.
2. **Jim Hopper** – Gruff yet caring local police chief who steps up as a protector.
3. **Dustin Henderson** – Energetic, witty, and loyal member of the group.
4. **Steve Harrington** – One of the best character arcs in the show, going from cocky jock to reliable babysitter and protector.
